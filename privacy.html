Privacy policy

WanderHQ · last updated 2026-11-05

Who uses WanderHQ

WanderHQ is a personal tool that runs on its author's own computer and is used only by its author. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API): the author's own channels — channel statistics, the list of uploaded videos and their statistics, comments on those videos; uploading the author's own videos when the author schedules them.
  • Instagram (via Instagram Graph API): the author's own professional accounts — account statistics, the list of the author's own Reels and their statistics; publishing the author's own Reels when the author schedules them.

WanderHQ does not access accounts, channels or data of any other person.

Where data is stored

All data and access tokens are stored only on the author's own computer, in a local database and local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official Google and Meta APIs that WanderHQ calls.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party.

Use of Google user data

WanderHQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. WanderHQ uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions and in Facebook / Instagram settings under connected apps. Local data is deleted by deleting the local database.
