WanderHQ

A personal, self-hosted tool. Not a public service.

WanderHQ runs on its author's own computer. It is used only by its author to:

  • collect statistics (views, likes, comments, subscribers) of the author's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts;
  • compare these statistics across the author's channels and videos;
  • schedule and publish the author's own videos to the author's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts.

WanderHQ has no other users, does not offer sign-up, and does not access anyone else's accounts.
